A new [policy forum(https://www.science.org/doi/epdf/10.1126/science.aea7431)) out in _Science_ today highlights when and how economic markets can be a promising tool for effective and efficient adaptation to climate change. **Dr. Kimberly Oremus** and the other 21 authors, who span various specialties within environmental economics, were originally brought together as contributors to the economics chapter of the U.S. Fifth National Climate Assessment.
