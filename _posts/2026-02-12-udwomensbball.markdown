The Birkenbach-Oremus Lab turned out to support UD Women's Basketball in one of their last games of the season and was treated to an exciting game against New Mexico State University. In spite of a demoralizing half-court, buzzer-beater basket by the Aggies to end the first half, the Hens rallied to keep a slim lead in the second. **Kent Wicker** scored a free t-shirt, and all the students got free backwards/upside-down Delaware hats to confuse their friends with. Another fun outing in the books!
