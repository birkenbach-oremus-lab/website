The Birkenbach-Oremus Lab braved a chilly night to support UD Women's Basketball in one of their last games of the season and was treated to an exciting game against New Mexico State University. In spite of a demoralizing half-court buzzer-beater by the Aggies to end the first half, the Hens rallied to keep a slim lead in the second. **Kent Wicker**, **Whitney Morgan**, and **Erinn Bell** are pictured above in their free and somewhat confusing Delaware hats, and Kent scored a free t-shirt as well. Nachos, cotton candy, popcorn, and fun were had by all!
