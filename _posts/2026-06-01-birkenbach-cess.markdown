**Dr. Anna Birkenbach** is excited to serve as Delaware's new appointee to the [Atlantic States Marine Fisheries Commission's Committee on Economics and Social Sciences (CESS)](https://asmfc.org/programs/science/economics/). Joining other experts spanning state, federal, and academic backgrounds, Dr. Birkenbach will further the integration of socioeconomic data and methods into fisheries management process and guide the Interstate Fisheries Management Program (ISFMP) and the  Atlantic Coastal Cooperative Statistics Program (ACCSP) in developing ``fishery management plans that reflect economic and social dimensions.'' She will serve as the committee's stock lead for horseshoe crabs and coastal sharks, a category spanning several prohibited and commercial species, and attended her first meeting in Washington, D.C. in early August.
